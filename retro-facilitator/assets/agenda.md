# Retro agenda (fixed order, no skipping)

1. **What shipped** — one paragraph summarizing what actually went out the door. Link to the retro source artifacts (PRD, launch post, dashboard, comms thread). No editorializing — just the facts of what landed.
2. **What worked** — 3–5 bullets. Each bullet must tie to a *specific decision or practice* (e.g. "weekly cross-functional sync on Thursdays caught two scope conflicts early"), not a vague compliment.
3. **What surprised us** — 3–5 bullets. Neutral facts only. Format: "We expected X, we saw Y." No attribution of blame, no "should haves" — those belong in step 4.
4. **What we'd do differently** — 3–5 bullets. Each paired with the reasoning ("why this would help"). Forward-looking, not punitive.
5. **Action items** — concrete next steps. **Every item MUST have an owner placeholder (`<role>`) AND a due date.** No exceptions. Items without both are how retros decay into nothing.
