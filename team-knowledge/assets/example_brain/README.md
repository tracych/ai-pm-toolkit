# Example brain — Shoplit Recommendations

A small worked example of a team brain after ~3 weeks of operation. Fictional team and product (Shoplit is a made-up e-commerce app; any resemblance to a real product is coincidental).

Use this to see what the layout looks like with actual content. Compare your own brain's `CLAUDE.md`, `contributors/`, and `intake.md` against the corresponding files here.

## What you'll find

- `team-config.yaml` — filled-in source identifiers (fake)
- `CLAUDE.md` — team brain after one moderation cycle (Goal/Strategy filled in by a human, This Week / Decisions / Active Projects auto-populated)
- `contributors/` — three teammates with sample Deliverables / Decisions / Blockers
- `intake.md` — two proposed additions awaiting moderation (one HIGH, one MED)
- `conflict_log.md` — one open conflict where two contributors gave different launch dates
- `reports/` — empty (would normally contain the moderation paper trail)
