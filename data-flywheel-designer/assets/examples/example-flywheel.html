1

Cold-start data plan

~5,000 (diff, expert-comment) pairs from a 40/40/20 mix: 2k mined from 12 months of internal PRs where the comment had a linked code change; 2k synthetic from a prompted GPT-class baseline filtered by a senior engineer; 1k hand-curated by 3 staff engineers covering concurrency / security / perf. v0 ships as the prompted baseline; fine-tune ships at week 6.
Skip this and the flywheel never spins — no v0 = no signal.
2

Signal capture

One event per generated comment. Explicit: thumbs-up/down + optional reason. Implicit: time_to_action_ms, accepted_without_edit, edit_distance_if_edited, dismissed_in_under_2s. Derived: pr_merged_with_comment_addressed (joined 24h later), same_file_re_review_in_7d. edit_distance is the single most informative signal.
Always include at least one implicit signal — explicit feedback is <1%.
3

Labeling pipeline

Three tiers. Tier 1 (auto, ~80% of events): accepted_without_edit → positive, dismissed_in_under_2s → hard negative. Tier 2 (active learning, ~400/wk): ambiguous edits + dwelled-then-dismissed go to a human queue. Tier 3 (gold, 50/wk): double-labeled by seniors, inter-rater agreement tracked weekly. Cost: ~450 labels/wk × 3 min × $1.50/min = $2,000/wk ($110k/yr). Owner: code-review platform team, 6 engineers × 15 min/day rotation.
No $/label number = hope, not plan.
4

Storage & schema

Hive table code_review_ai_events, one row per generated comment. Fields: request_id, pr_id, repo_id, file_path_hash, diff_hunk, model_version, prompt_template_version, generated_comment, suggested_edit_block, user_action enum, time_to_action_ms, accepted_without_edit, edit_distance_if_edited, final_edit_block_if_edited, derived_signals struct, timestamp, reviewer_user_id_hashed. 18-month hot retention, then aggregates only. PII: hashed user IDs, file paths hashed (can contain usernames), no PR titles/descriptions.
Missing fields can't be backfilled six months later.
5

Retraining trigger

Fine-tune fires when ANY of: (a) 5,000 new Tier-1+Tier-2 labels accumulate, OR (b) eval-set acceptance-rate drops 4pp vs deployed, OR (c) 8 weeks elapsed since last run. Daily cron opens a JIRA in the platform team queue and pages on-call. First retrain expected ~week 10 post-launch (5k labels at ~700/wk).
Quantified — "N samples OR K-pp eval drop", not "when ready".
6

Closing the loop

Three-stage rollout. (1) Shadow: new model runs on 100% of traffic, output hidden, 1 week of offline scoring vs live. (2) Canary A/B: 5% of PRs for 1 week, guardrail = accepted_without_edit rate, auto-rollback if it drops 3pp at p<0.05. (3) Ramp: 5% → 25% → 100% over 2 weeks. Permanent 1% holdout on v0 prompted baseline for long-term flywheel-effect measurement. Rollback owner: platform on-call.
Shadow / A/B / ramp — never auto-deploy a fresh model.
